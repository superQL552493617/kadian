FAQ

Where is my data stored?
Everything stays on your phone; we have no servers. You can export a backup in the app.

How do I restore Premium?
Tap “Restore purchases” on the Premium page using the same Apple ID.

How do I cancel auto-renewal?
Turn it off in iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

Contact us

Tap “Feedback” in the app. We’ll get back to you soon.
